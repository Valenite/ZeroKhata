## 🎯 1. The Real-World Problem We Solved
Over **80 million micro-entrepreneurs** (home bakers, custom apparel makers, cloud kitchens, freelance tutors) run their business almost entirely over **WhatsApp and Instagram DMs**.

**Their daily nightmare:**
1. **Lost Revenue & Uncollected Credit (Udhaar):** Customers pay a small advance and promise the rest on delivery, but forget. Business owners lose 15–20% of their margin because chasing money repeatedly feels socially awkward.
2. **Order Chaos in Chats:** Delivery dates, cake weights, and dietary requirements ("eggless", "write Happy 25th Anita") get buried under 200 unread messages.
3. **No Official Invoices:** Informal text chats lack authority, causing customers to treat payments casually.
4. **Existing Tools Are Too Bloated:** Shopify and Zoho cost ₹2,000+/month, require 4 hours of setup, and demand GST and merchant accounts. Small sellers need a bicycle, not an airplane.

---

## 💡 2. The Solution: ZeroKhata
A zero-friction, WhatsApp-native digital operating system designed specifically for non-technical small business owners.

### 🌟 Core Working Features:
- **✨ AI Voice & Message Parser:**
  - Paste any unstructured Hinglish voice transcript or messy WhatsApp chat.
  - Automatically extracts: customer name, phone, delivery date/time, items, advance paid, and custom notes in **1.2 seconds**.
- **📲 1-Click WhatsApp Invoicing:**
  - Generates clean, emoji-formatted order invoices with order numbers, itemized breakdown, advance paid, and bold balance due.
  - One-tap dispatch directly to the customer's chat via official `wa.me` links.
- **🧾 Instant Printable Thermal Receipt & UPI QR:**
  - Printable/PDF bill with business header, itemized pricing, and UPI scan-to-pay QR code.
- **📊 Kitchen & Delivery Kanban Board:**
  - Track orders visually: `New` ➔ `In Kitchen / Prep` ➔ `Ready for Dispatch` ➔ `Delivered & Done` (with celebratory confetti).
- **📒 Digital Khata (Debt Book):**
  - Aggregates customer lifetime spend and pending market credit.
  - Includes a **1-click WhatsApp payment reminder** button that recovers money without awkwardness.
- **📦 Customizable Product & Price Catalog:**
  - Rapid 1-tap item insertion into new orders.
- **💾 Offline-Ready & Persistent:**
  - Full `localStorage` persistence with one-click **"Reset Demo Data"** for judges and testing.

## 💼 3. The Business Model & Unit Economics
- **Free Tier:** Up to 30 orders/month (100% free forever).
- **Pro Subscription (₹299 / $3.60 per month):**
  - Unlimited orders & AI Voice parsing.
  - Multi-staff kitchen sync (helpers view).
  - Automated WhatsApp payment reminders.
  - Custom UPI QR codes on receipts.
  - Monthly tax and profit/loss statements.
- **Unit Economics:** 10,000 Pro sellers = **₹3.6 Crore ARR ($430K)** with near-zero churn.
- **Viral Flywheel (Zero CAC):** Every invoice sent carries the footer: *"Billed via ZeroKhata — Free for small businesses"*. Buyers who run side-hustles click and sign up organically.

## 🚀 Running the Project Locally

ZeroKhata has **zero external dependencies**.
